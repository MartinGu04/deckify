The first public release of Deckify — a Spotify control center for Elgato Stream Deck.

## Highlights

- Live Spotify playback state directly on your Stream Deck
- Play / Pause, Previous and Next with local-first control
- Spotify-native volume and mute
- Like / Unlike tracks
- Add tracks to playlists with duplicate protection
- Launch playlists directly from your Stream Deck
- Spotify Connect support
- Guided Deckify Setup with OAuth + PKCE
- SIMPLE and DECKIFY starter experiences
- No telemetry and no Spotify Client Secret required

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or later
- Stream Deck 7.1+
- Spotify Premium
- Your own Spotify Client ID

## Install

Download `com.marting.deckify.streamDeckPlugin` from the assets below and double-click it to install.

**Install → Connect → Choose → Play.**
